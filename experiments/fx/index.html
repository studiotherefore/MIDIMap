      Effects

      A saved preset keeps the effects, blend, camera, playback and the current photo. Saving under an existing name updates it; × deletes.

      Presets sync: checking…

      Click learn, then move a MiniLab knob or fader to control that effect. Names follow Drift's look engine where they overlap.

      Capture

      Records only the picture (no panel, no cursor) to a video file in your Downloads folder. V starts and stops; the kick is recorded too. Open output window shows the picture alone in a separate window (drag it to a projector, then press F there for fullscreen); it follows this one.
