Keyboard controller

—

A pretend MIDI controller on the keyboard. It turns on by itself when no controller is connected and off when one is plugged in. Hold a knob key to turn it; add Shift for fine moves.

Mappings

Click Learn, then press a key or move a MIDI control. Knob/fader actions accept CC, pitch bend or aftertouch only. An endless knob (one that keeps turning, like the MiniLab's main knob) is recognised while learning and marked ∞; click ∞ / ⇥ on a mapping to switch it by hand.
